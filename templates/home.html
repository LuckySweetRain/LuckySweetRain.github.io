01CONTROL THEORY

Control theory

I study control methods for active suspension systems. I focus on stability and uncertainty.

Prescribed-time controlNonlinear systems
Research focus +

I study vertical and pitch motion in a half-car model. I compare time-varying feedback and periodic delayed sliding mode control in simulation.

02SAFE REINFORCEMENT LEARNING

Safe reinforcement
learning

I use road preview to help a suspension system plan ahead. I study how it can learn while respecting safety limits.

Safe RLRoad preview
Research focus +

My thesis uses road preview, Transformer policies, and PPO. I study safe-teacher initialization and action shielding. This work is ongoing.
